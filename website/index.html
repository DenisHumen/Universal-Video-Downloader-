Everything in one window

Paste a link

More than 1800 sites work directly. For the rest the app reads the page, or opens it in a hidden browser, and finds the stream itself. Choose video or sound only, the quality, subtitles, and the part of the video you want.

Search by title

Type what you are looking for instead of a link: YouTube, SoundCloud, Dailymotion, Bilibili, Niconico and anime catalogues at once, with the best quality of each result shown before you download.

A queue that looks after itself

Several downloads at once, pause and resume, a retry when the connection drops, and a plain sentence with a button that fixes it when something goes wrong. Quit in the middle and it carries on next time.

New episodes by themselves

Follow a series, a channel or a playlist. New episodes are found on a schedule, downloaded, renamed, sent to a network folder and announced in Telegram, while the app sits in the tray.

Your library

Everything you have downloaded, by date or by site, with play, show in folder, trim and convert. Moved a folder? One Locate finds the rest.

And also

  • Sign in onceMembers-only and age-gated videos: sign in in the built-in browser, or bring the sign-ins over from your own browser.
  • Convert and trimMP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, GIF, or sound as MP3, FLAC and Opus. Cut a clip before or after downloading.
  • A terminal commanduvd <link> downloads with the same settings as the app, for scripts and quick jobs.
  • A browser extensionSend the page you are on, or a link, to the app with one click.
  • Looks at homeDesigned after GNOME on Ubuntu, with a day and a night theme and ten accent colours. The same on Windows and macOS.
  • Nothing sent without youNo account, no ads, no tracking. An error report is only ever sent when you press the button, and you see what is in it.

Install from a repository and update with the system

Signed repositories: updates arrive with sudo apt upgrade or sudo dnf upgrade.

curl -fsSL https://denishumen.github.io/Universal-Video-Downloader/uvd.gpg | sudo tee /usr/share/keyrings/uvd.gpg > /dev/null
echo "deb [arch=amd64 signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/uvd.gpg] https://github.com/DenisHumen/Universal-Video-Downloader/releases/latest/download/ ./" | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/universal-video-downloader.list
sudo apt update && sudo apt install universal-video-downloader
sudo curl -fsSL -o /etc/yum.repos.d/universal-video-downloader.repo https://denishumen.github.io/Universal-Video-Downloader/universal-video-downloader.repo
sudo dnf install universal-video-downloader
